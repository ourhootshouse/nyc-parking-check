How I read the sign

Citywide rules (no sign needed)

  • No parking within 15 ft of a fire hydrant (even if unmarked).
  • Don't block crosswalks, sidewalks, or driveways (including your host's).
  • No standing at bus stops, or double parking.
  • Meters: pay with the ParkNYC app or the muni-meter; they are suspended on major legal holidays.
  • Street cleaning (broom) rules are suspended on ASP holidays; other signs usually still apply.
Official sources: NYC DOT Parking · DOT parking sign guide · DOF violation codes · ASP calendar · 311 ASP · ParkNYC

⚠️ This is a helper, not a legal guarantee. Photo text reading can be wrong, so always compare it with the actual sign. When more than one sign is on a pole, the most restrictive one applies.
